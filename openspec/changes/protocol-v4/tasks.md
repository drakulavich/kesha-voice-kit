## 4. Carrier release

- [x] 4.1 Tag `v1.25.0-beta.1`, un-draft by hand, verify `kesha install --engine-version 1.25.0-beta.1` downloads it
- [x] 4.2 Remove the `KESHA_PROTOCOL` window and cut `v1.25.0-beta.2`. What went, and what it dragged with it:
  - the v3 renderer — `Mode`, `events::mode()`, `mode_for` and the `Mode::V3` arm of `Event::render`, which now takes no argument. `KESHA_PROTOCOL` is read nowhere in `rust/`: the engine speaks protocol 4 whatever the environment says
  - `--capabilities-json` and `--error-codes-json`, and with them `errors::error_codes_json` and its `CodeEntry`. `capabilities::get_capabilities` stays because `describe` consumes it, but `Capabilities` lost `protocol_version` (and its `Serialize` derive) — nothing serialised the struct once the flag was gone, and the field still said `3`
  - the `KESHA_DEBUG_FD` sink in `debug.rs` — `json_sink`, `JSON_SINK`, `json_sink_is_active` and `trace_json`'s fd branch. `dtrace_json!` now gates on `debug::enabled()`, so its six call sites emit `debug` events unchanged
  - **the terminal progress bar and the recording row ticker, because they only ever painted under the mode.** `ProgressReader` lost `bar_paints`, `BAR_LINE_OPEN`, `lock_stderr`, `end_open_bar_line`, `PROGRESS_BAR_WIDTH` and its `Drop` impl; `reader_wanted` reduced to the byte floor and `progress_event` to the in-flight rule. `ListenTicker` lost `Tick`, `output` and `row_open`, keeping only the once-per-second dedupe. `with_stderr` went with the bar it existed to protect, so its seven call sites emit directly instead: five in `download.rs` (four `events::progress`, one `secondary_failure(..).emit()`), one in `staging.rs`, and one `events::warn` in `models/paths.rs` under `#[cfg(feature = "coreml")]` — the site a default `cargo clippy --all-targets` cannot see, and the one that broke during this change
  - the tests that pinned the window. `describe_cli.rs`'s two `…_still_answers_during_the_window` cases became one `the_legacy_flags_are_gone` case asserting exit 2 and an `E_INVALID_ARG` event — a positive guard beats an absence. `debug_events_v4.rs` lost `v3_debug_trace_is_unchanged` and is now `debug_events.rs`; `protocol_events_cli.rs` lost its `[None, Some("4")]` loops; `capabilities.rs` lost `protocol_version_is_3`; `record.rs`, `models/progress.rs` and `models/download.rs` lost their `Mode::V3` cases
  - `rust/tests/no_stray_eprintln.rs`'s `ALLOWED` ledger, down from seven entries to one (`events.rs`, the emitter itself). Six of the seven named the painter and the bar, in prose no symbol search could reach
  - **`build-engine.yml`'s smoke step moved to `describe`** in the same commit, with `CLAUDE.md`, `docs/architecture.md`, `docs/tts.md` and the `release-engine` / `release-mechanics` skills, all of which told a maintainer to run a flag that no longer parses
  - the tag: `v1.25.0-beta.2` was cut from `main` and became the pin at #1184, ahead of 5.3 rather than after it — see the ordering note there for why `check-versions` settled that for us
